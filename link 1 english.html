THE MV WILHELM GUSTLOFF 1945


The MV Wilhelm Gustloff was a passenger ship in Nazi Germany.


The MV Wilhelm Gustloff was 208.5 meters long and 23.50 – 23.59 meters wide.


The MV Wilhelm Gustloff had a speed of 15.5 knots; with 9,500 hp, that equals 29 km/h.


The construction period of the MV Wilhelm Gustloff spanned 9 to 10 months.


The name MV Wilhelm Gustloff came from the leading member of the NSDAP foreign organization in Switzerland (NSDAP = National Socialist German Workers' Party).


The MV Wilhelm Gustloff was commissioned in 1937 and was in service for civilian cruises for 2 years.

On September 1, 1939, the MV Wilhelm Gustloff was converted into a military warship, which was in service from 1939 to 1945.

The MV Wilhelm Gustloff was operated under the ownership of the "Strength Through Joy" organization (KdF). It also belonged to the German Labour Front.
